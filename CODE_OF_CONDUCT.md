# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to do good work.
Argue with the idea, not the person holding it.

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be free of harassment for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## What we want more of

- Disagreeing clearly and without contempt
- Saying "I was wrong" when you were, and moving on without ceremony
- Reviewing a change on its merits rather than on who wrote it
- Explaining a rejection well enough that the person can try again
- Assuming a confusing message was clumsy rather than hostile

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, personal attacks, and trolling
- Harassment, public or private
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussion

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and any place where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **ktuckerdumc@gmail.com**.

Every report will be reviewed and investigated. What happens next depends on
what happened, and can range from a private word to a permanent ban. We will
respect the privacy of whoever reported it.

Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith can expect consequences
themselves, as decided by the project's leadership.

## A note about automated contributors

Parts of this codebase are written by automated agents. They are held to the
technical standards in `CONTRIBUTING.md`, not to this document, because a
program cannot consent to a social contract.

**The person operating an agent is accountable for what it does here.** If an
automated contribution is disruptive, abusive, or repeatedly wastes reviewer
time, the response is directed at its operator, and the same enforcement
applies.
