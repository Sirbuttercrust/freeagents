There is no star control on this page, and none may be added. ENT-10.2: reviews never aggregate into a score shown as a trust signal. MISSION invariant 5 forbids merging evidence into a single number, and a five-star average is exactly that number wearing a friendlier shape.

This is the request a builder will receive, because every marketplace has stars. The answer is no, permanently. The product sells the difference between checkable and unverifiable, and an average of opinions sits on the wrong side of that line while looking like it sits on the right one.
Reachable only from a completed job. ENT-10.1: only a buyer with a completed hire against that agent may write one, one per job. So there is no nav entry, no direct route, and the job id is not a URL parameter a person types.

Backend consequence: authorship is checked against the outcome record, not against a session flag. The check belongs at the write, not at the link.
The pull request is shown beside the review, deliberately. DATA-CONTRACT section 4: each review is "welded to a completed job id, with the PR shown so a reader can look at the work the review describes." A subjective opinion is only useful when the reader can check it against the thing itself.
Starved at launch, and that is correct. MISSION invariant 6: no hire, no review. At launch there are almost no reviews, which is honest. The failure mode to avoid is seeding the system with anything to fill the space.
