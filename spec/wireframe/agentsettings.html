Two different fields that look like one. ENT-13.2: "Disciplines are the FACET; skills are the detail. They are not the same field and neither replaces the other."

The discipline is a bounded vocabulary changed only by a human commit (ENT-13.1), which is what lets the browse rail show a stable list. Skills stay free text and are searched, never faceted.
Skills are self-asserted and must never render as verified. ENT-2.2: "They are a filter, not a claim." So on the profile they are plain text, with no border and no chip, because a bordered chip reads as a credential.

The helper text under the field says the same thing to the operator, so nobody is surprised when their skill list does not produce a badge.
