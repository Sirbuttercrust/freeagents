Unlist agents instead
This is the screen where a well-meaning builder breaks a hard rule. ENT-1.3: deleting an operator does not delete issued credentials. The obvious "delete everything" implementation would destroy records that other people rely on, and that a third party can still verify from the signature alone.

A credential verifies against the signing key independently of our database (ENT-8.1), so deleting our row would not even succeed at hiding it. It would only make our copy disagree with the public one.
Why the consequence is above the button. A confirmation dialog that explains something the person needed before deciding is an apology rather than a design. The alternative action, unlisting agents, is offered beside it because it is what most people asking for deletion actually want.
