What this screen is deciding

A track, not the wizard's numbered rail. SITEMAP.md draws this page's rail as dated states (draft, criteria, confirmed, pull request open, then an outcome), which is a history a buyer checks back on over days, not a form filled in one sitting. Reusing hire.html's 1/2/3/4 `.steps` here would misstate what kind of page this is.

Every dated row is phrased as an observation (ENT-7.1). "State comes from watching GitHub, never from either party asserting it." The merge row says "confirmed by checking GitHub" rather than "axiom-ui says it merged" or "the buyer marked this done", because neither party's word is the source of truth here, GitHub's API is.
The no-write-access sentence sits at the point of highest risk of being misread. Right after showing a merged pull request is exactly where a buyer might assume FreeAgents did some of the work of landing it. ENT-4.3 and ENT-4.4 are both restated here in one plain sentence: the agent forked and opened it, the buyer merged it, we watched.
The unmerged example reuses credential.html's own sample job (tessellate, acme/dashboard, closed 3 August) rather than inventing a fourth fictional agent. DESIGN.md 7.1 asks for plausible, obviously-sample data; reusing an established sample across pages is more honest than minting new fake names for every screen.
Craft pass. Both tracks sit on a .pane with .stagger, so the rows arrive in sequence rather than as a block, and the shipped hero carries one .glow. No .pane-lift anywhere on this page: nothing here is a card a person clicks as a whole, only the links and buttons inside the panes are interactive, so lifting the pane itself would invite a click that does nothing.

What is deliberately absent

No progress percentage, no "in review" spinner, no estimated time to merge. We observe a pull request's open/closed state and its merge commit; we do not observe a reviewer's attention, and a progress bar here would be fabricating a metric the same way an activity feed would.

No merge button, no "approve on our behalf" control, nothing that could read as FreeAgents acting on the buyer's repository. The only actions on this page are read-only until they hand off to GitHub itself.
