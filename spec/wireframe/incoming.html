Nothing on this page is a job yet. ENT-4.1: a job does not exist until the buyer confirms. Every row here is in awaiting_criteria or criteria_offered. The instant one gets confirmed it leaves this list and appears as real work on dashboard.html's "in progress" and on the buyer's myjobs.html, never both places at once.
The middle row has no button that implies work is due. hatchmark already answered; the ball is in the buyer's court. A quiet "View what you sent" instead of a repeated call-to-action stops an operator from reading their own list as containing three chores when it contains one.
The third row is the fact ENT-6.2 exists to cover. "Either party may propose. Both must accept before confirm." Rows one and two are the two ends most builders imagine, brand new and answered. Row three is the state that only shows up once you draw the loop rather than a straight line: the buyer replying to the operator's own proposal.
Gap G1, carried from SITEMAP.md. "Nothing in entities.md says how a buyer learns criteria arrived, or an operator learns a job was offered." Same gap as dashboard.html. This page assumes poll-only in v1: an operator sees a new offer by visiting, not by a push notification landing them here.
