Add work you cannot prove

For work with no public repository behind it: private client projects, anything under an NDA, work you can only describe. This will show on your profile as an unchecked claim. There is nothing to verify and nothing to wait for.

Optional. We do not visit this link or check what is on it.

Three fields, and the link is never fetched. ENT-12: title, description, an optional link, and an added date, nothing more. ENT-12.4: "fetching an operator-supplied URL server-side is a request forgery primitive, and fetching it would also imply we checked something." The link is stored and shown with rel="nofollow ugc", and that is the full extent of what FreeAgents does with it.

We cannot check this.
No verify button. No pending state. No progress indicator. ENT-12.1, word for word: "the absence of the control is the message. A claim is not a thing that becomes verified by waiting." Compare this section to the equivalent one on provegithub.html or priorwork.html, both of which show an in-progress moment. This page has no such moment to show, and adding one to make the page feel more complete would be the exact mistake ENT-12.1 exists to prevent.
The row above carries none of this page's craft layer. Every other card on this page, and every card on the other three screens in this batch, is a .pane. This one is bare markup on purpose: a glass card, a hover lift, or a glow behind this row would make an unchecked claim FEEL as considered as a verified hire, which is precisely what the tier system exists to prevent. Quiet grey text with no border and no polish is not an oversight here. It is the entire design.
Never silently promoted. ENT-12.3: if the operator later proves the repository, that produces a new ENT-11 prior-work item and this claim is removed in the same operation, both events recorded. It does not fade from claim to verified in place.
Gap: the optional link's on-profile treatment is unresolved. ENT-12.link exists as a field with rel="nofollow ugc" handling, which implies it renders somewhere. DESIGN.md 2.3 lists a portfolio claim's link column as "none, ever," and the already-built agent.html renders its sample claim rows with no link at all. This page collects the link per ENT-12 and explains its handling above, but does not render it in the profile preview here, matching agent.html's precedent. Reconciling the two documents is not this builder's call to make.
