# FreeAgents brand

The rules from `brand.html`, as plain text for the site repo. Every file named here is in this kit and is generated by `build/make.py kit`. Edit the build, never the files.

## The logo

A character peeks over the name from the valley between the F and the A, looking toward "Agents". The lockup is the name and the character together. The character on its own is the icon, at every size.

- On dark backgrounds: `logo/freeagents-logo-dark.svg`.
- On light backgrounds: `logo/freeagents-logo-light.svg`.
- One colour: `logo/freeagents-logo-black.svg` (#08090A), `logo/freeagents-logo-white.svg` (#FFFFFF), `logo/freeagents-logo-mono.svg` (takes the CSS colour through `currentColor`).
- The icon: `logo/freeagents-icon-dark.svg`, `-light.svg`, `-mono.svg`.
- The wordmark alone, for places too small or too formal for the character: `logo/freeagents-wordmark.svg` (`currentColor`).
- PNG lockups, transparent, 600 px wide at 1x with @2x and @4x: `logo/png/`.

## Clear space

The unit is E, the height of the character's eye: 192.0 units against a cap height of 710, so E is 0.27 of the cap height. On the 600 px PNG, E is 21.6 px.

- Keep 2E clear on every side of the lockup.
- Keep 1E clear around the icon.
- Measure from the outermost ink (the tip of an ear, the tail of the g). Nothing else goes in that space.

Drawn in `guide/clearspace-lockup.svg` and `guide/clearspace-icon.svg`.

## Minimum sizes

Measured by `build/kit.py`: each file rendered in Chrome at 1x on a ladder of widths, reading the pixel under each feature (eyes dark with body between, ears body with a dark notch between, and for one-colour files a dark line between head and letter). The minimum is the smallest width from which every larger step passed. Raw results: `guide/measurements.json`.

| File | Minimum width | Largest failing width |
|---|---|---|
| Lockup, colour | 80 px | 70 px |
| Lockup, one colour | 230 px | 220 px |
| Icon, colour | 18 px | 16 px |
| Icon, one colour | 42 px | 40 px |

Below the lockup's minimum, use the wordmark or the icon. At 16 and 32 px, use the drawn favicon files, not a scaled icon.

## Colour

WCAG 2 contrast ratios. The logo is a graphic, so it needs 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11).

| Colour | Hex | On #08090A | On #FFFFFF | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Character blue | `#2B8CFF` | 5.98:1 | 3.33:1 | The character. The one colour it comes in. |
| Ink | `#08090A` | 1.00:1 | 19.93:1 | Letters on light backgrounds, the dark background, the eyes. |
| Paper | `#F7F8F8` | 18.73:1 | 1.06:1 | Letters on dark backgrounds. The same value as the site's text colour. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | 19.93:1 | 1.00:1 | The one-colour white file, light backgrounds. |

The gloss on the character uses a highlight `#9CD6FF`, a shade `#1450D6` and a rim `#0E3FB0`. They belong to the drawing and are never used on their own.

`#7C7CFF` is not a logo colour. It is the site's `--accent`, which DESIGN.md section 2.2 reserves for "verified" (5.87:1 on #08090A, 3.40:1 on #FFFFFF). It stays off the logo so the colour keeps one meaning.

## Type

- Wordmark: Geist SemiBold, weight 650, tracked -0.02em, kerned, converted to outlines. The logo files need no font installed.
- Lines set beside the logo (the tagline, the domain on the social images): Geist, weight 500.
- Geist: Version 1.401, Copyright 2024 The Geist Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1, full text in `type/OFL.txt`.

## Favicon and app icons

The 16 and 32 px icons are drawn for their size (flat blue, square ears with a clear notch, larger eyes, the ledge), because the glossy drawing turns to mush at 16 px. `favicon.svg` is the 16 px drawing as vectors.

- `favicon/favicon.svg`, `favicon/favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48)
- `favicon/apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `favicon/icon-192.png`, `favicon/icon-512.png`
- `favicon/icon-512-maskable.png`: the character inside the central 80%, so any mask keeps it whole
- `favicon/site.webmanifest` names the three app icons

With the favicon files at the site root:

```html
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="32x32">
<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">
<link rel="manifest" href="/site.webmanifest">
```

## What not to do

- Stretch or squash it. Scale it from the corner, both ways at once. (`guide/dont-stretch.svg`)
- Recolour the character outside the palette. The character is #2B8CFF. The one shown is #7C7CFF, the site's "verified" colour, which is never a logo colour. (`guide/dont-recolour.svg`)
- Add a drop shadow, glow or outline. The mark sits flat on its background. (`guide/dont-shadow.svg`)
- Rotate the lockup. It stays level. (`guide/dont-rotate.svg`)
- Move the character to another letter. It peeks from the valley between the F and the A and looks toward "Agents". (`guide/dont-move.svg`)
- Put the dark lockup on a light background. The light letters disappear. Use freeagents-logo-light.svg on light backgrounds. (`guide/dont-dark-on-light.svg`)
