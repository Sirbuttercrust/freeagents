What this screen is deciding

The buyer pays for work they have not seen, and the attestation is the entire compensation for that. Every field it carries was argued over: the buyer wanted per-criterion file mapping and raw test output, and both were refused because they publish the architecture and leak symbol names before payment. Failing test names survived, because those are the buyer's own text from the buyer's own suite.

No verdict, no colour, no ordering by concern. "2 tests newly skipped" and "0 files outside the agreed paths" are rendered identically, because deciding which of those is worrying is a judgement about the work. A red chip on the skipped tests would be the platform arbitrating, which it has refused to do. The buyer reads nine facts and decides.

Note what the fixed order buys: the same nine rows appear on every hire, so a buyer who has read one of these can read the next in five seconds, and an operator cannot present a flattering subset.
Three buttons, and the count is a rule. Pay, redo, decline. Every candidate fourth control moves the deal somewhere nothing can verify: a message thread replaces the agreement with a chat, an extend-the-clock button reopens a term both parties signed, an ask-a-question control is a message thread with better manners.
The redo cites a line, and the list is the agreed lines only. A free-text redo would let a buyer send work back over something neither side ever agreed to, which is unanswerable for the operator and meaningless on the record. Line 06, the price, is absent from the picker: a redo is about the work, and the price was settled at the lock.
The clock is a date and a consequence, not a draining bar. A meter emptying while a person reads is pressure applied by the venue, and this venue does not lean on a buyer to decide faster. What happens at zero is stated beside the date, because a deadline with an unnamed consequence is just anxiety.
Decline says exactly what it costs, before the click. Five rows: what you pay, where the deposit goes, what happens to the code, and one row per record. "Free" is true and incomplete on its own, because the deposit is already gone, and a buyer who learns that afterwards was misled by a word that was technically accurate.

What is deliberately absent

No preview of the code, no file tree that expands, no "peek at one file". Any of them makes the staging gate leak, and the gate is what makes unpaid work impossible to take.

No quality signal of any kind: no diff-size judgement, no complexity score, no "this looks small for $1,200". MISSION forbids any automated quality score derived from our own opinion of a diff.
