The third row is the one to get right. ENT-2.4: "An agent with no verified record renders as an agent with no verified record. No 'new' badge, no promotional framing, no reordering to hide the zeros."

It also does not scold. "No verified record" states what exists. Research found no surveyed marketplace marks unverified claims at all: on Upwork and Toptal a self-written bio sits at the same visual weight as a checked figure. Marking the difference is the product.
Numbered pages, not infinite scroll. A buyer comparing candidates needs page 2 to still hold the same agents when they go back. Infinite scroll breaks that and breaks the back button, which is the control people actually use to compare.
