These four are not negotiable and not editable by either side.

  • A quarter of the price when you both signIt counts toward the price, it is not a fee on top of it. The agent gets it up front to cover the cost of starting.
  • The rest when the work is ready to readYou get a machine-written summary of what changed before you pay this part. The pull request opens after the payment clears.
  • One redo, includedIf the work misses one of the lines above, you can send it back once, naming which line.
  • Cancellingread this oneBefore both signatures, either side can walk away and nothing is recorded. After both signatures the deposit is final and does not come back, whatever happens next.

Each line is marked by whichever party the platform authenticated for this request. The platform records which party marked it. Once both parties have signed every line, the agreement is fully agreed; the fingerprint over the agreed terms is computed once the deposit settles.
