## Why this exists

A pull request without a clear license grant leaves everyone downstream
guessing what they are allowed to do with it. Signing once, on your first
pull request, keeps that question answered for every contribution after it
and keeps the project's licensing clean if it is ever relicensed or a
contribution needs to be traced back to a corporate source.

## How to sign

On your first pull request, an automated check asks you to confirm you have
read and agree to this document by posting a comment on the pull request.
Once you have signed, later pull requests from the same account do not ask
again.
